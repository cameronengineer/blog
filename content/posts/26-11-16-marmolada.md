On 9 August, during my trip to Europe, I climbed Marmolada in the Italian Dolomites. It was a big day: **22.4 kilometres, 1,887 metres of ascent, and 10 hours 34 minutes out and back**.

I started near Penìa at about 1,500 metres and made my way up to Punta Penia, before returning to almost exactly the same spot. My recording tops out at 3,349 metres. That is the watch’s elevation reading, rather than a surveyed height for the summit.

The elevation profile tells the story pretty well: a long climb out of the valley, a slower section high on the mountain, and then all that height to lose again on the way down.
